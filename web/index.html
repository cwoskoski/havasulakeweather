About this weather station

Havasu Lake Weather shows live outdoor conditions for Havasu Lake, California — the small community in San Bernardino County on the California (west) shore of Lake Havasu, across the water from Lake Havasu City, Arizona. To be clear: this is Havasu Lake, CA (ZIP 92363), not Lake Havasu City, AZ.

Readings come from a personal Ambient Weather WS-2902D station in Havasu Lake and refresh about once a minute. You'll find the current temperature and feels-like, wind speed, gusts and direction, whether it's raining right now, plus humidity, dew point, UV index, barometric pressure and today's rainfall — with 24-hour and 7-day history charts.

A local resident keeps the station running for neighbors and lake-goers planning boating, fishing and desert-summer days on the water. It's for community reference only and is not an official National Weather Service observation.
